How to use the app

A short guide: from the first sign-in to SOS.

1. First sign-in

  1. Open the app and sign in with Apple or Google.
  2. Create a family: enter your name and the family name. Or choose «Enter code» and join a family with the code from the organizer.
  3. On the «Family created» screen you can add a child and invite a second parent right away.

2. Family and roles

The «Family» tab: members, invitations and settings (the gear).

  • Organizer — the creator of the family; manages members and roles.
  • Parent — full access to the family.
  • Helper (nanny, grandparent) — sees the schedule and helps with tasks; only views in «Lite» mode.
  • Child — sees their own events and tasks.

Invite an adult

«Family» → «Invitation» → choose «Parent» or «Helper» → «Create invite code» → send the code (the «Share» button). The code is valid for 7 days and for one person. They enter it at the first sign-in («Enter code»).

Add a child

«Family» → «Add child»: name, date of birth, food restrictions (used in the menu), avatar. If the child has their own phone, open their card → «Invite to a device» and give the code to the child: they enter it at the first sign-in and see only their own events and tasks. You can disconnect the device in the same card.

3. Calendar and events

  • The «Calendar» tab: the week on top, the chosen day's events below.
  • «+» — a new event: title, time, who it is for, address, repeat, reminder.
  • If an address is given, the app finds the point on the map and the event gets a route button.
  • The «Drop-off and pick-up» block: assign who takes the child and who picks them up. That person gets a reminder a set time before the start or the end.
  • Deleting a repeating event: «only this day», «this and all following» or «the whole series». Any deletion can be undone within a few seconds.

4. Tasks

On «Today» and in the «Calendar» there is the «Tasks» block: «+» creates an assignment (who, due date). Tap the circle to mark a task done; you can delete it with a swipe.

5. Shopping list

The «Shopping» tab: add items by hand; the list is shared and updates for everyone at once. Ingredients from the weekly menu are added with the «To shopping» button.

6. Menu and recipes

  • The «Menu» tab: the week on top (swipe sideways), the dishes of the chosen day below. «Add dish» takes a dish from the recipe book or creates a new one; a meal can have several dishes.
  • The recipe book (the book icon): your own recipes with ingredients and steps.
  • Plan the menu with AI (the sparkles button): choose the week and the days (even in different weeks), the meals and your wishes (quick, no fish, etc.; the field can be filled by voice). New dishes are added to those already planned, nothing is replaced. The AI takes family members' food restrictions into account. Always check AI recipes yourself, especially when there are allergies.

7. The SOS button

On «Today» there is a red button with a bell. Tap it and confirm «Send the signal»: your whole family gets a call for help with your current location (one point, no tracking). A red screen with a siren and an «Open on map» button opens on their phones. You can send it at most once a minute.

Important. SOS is a signal to your loved ones, not a call to emergency services. In danger, call 112 (or your local emergency number).

For the signal to always arrive, turn notifications on for the app on every family member's phone (Settings → Notifications). «Do Not Disturb» and Focus modes can silence ordinary notifications: add the app to the exceptions.

8. Reminders and notifications

If notifications are off, «Today» shows a card with a «Turn on» or «Settings» button. How many minutes ahead you are reminded is set in the event, and for those who accompany in the «Family» section.

9. Voice input

Multi-line fields (for example «Your wishes» for the menu) have a microphone icon: tap it and dictate. On first use iOS asks for microphone and speech recognition permission. The app does not keep the recording.

10. Settings

«Family» → the gear: profile (name, avatar), «Family+», preferences (theme, app language, the first day of the week), sign-in (connect Apple or Google so you do not lose access when you change phones).

11. The «Family+» subscription

For the first 30 days everything is available. After that «Lite» mode (up to 10 active events, no «Drop-off and pick-up», no AI menu, etc.) or «Family+» without limits. One person subscribes, the whole family uses it. Manage and cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings. «Settings» → «Family+» → «Restore purchases» brings the subscription back after a phone change.

Frequently asked questions

The SOS signal or a reminder does not arrive

Check: notifications are on for the app; «Do Not Disturb» or Focus has no exception; the phone is online; the app is up to date and you are signed in under your own account in this family.

How to change phones

Sign in with the same method (Apple or Google) and the family opens. Children connected by a code get a new code from a parent («Invite to a device»).

How to delete the account and data

Write to the address on the «Support» page: we will delete the account and the related data.
