Famlio

One shared calendar, tasks, shopping list and weekly menu for the whole family. Parents, children and helpers see what they need, and nobody forgets anything.

What the app does

Shared calendar

Events with repeats, an address and reminders. Mark who the event is for, and who drops off and picks up the child: the reminder arrives in advance, travel time included.

Tasks

Assignments for each family member with due dates. Tasks show in the calendar and can be marked done.

Shopping list

One list that updates for the whole family at once. Ingredients from the weekly menu go to the list in one tap.

Menu and recipes

A weekly menu, your own recipe book, and AI suggestions that take each family member's food restrictions into account.

Kids with their own phone

A child signs in with a code and sees only their own events and tasks. Parents see the schedule of children without a phone.

SOS button

One tap sends your family a call for help with your location: a red screen and a siren on their phones.

Who it is for

Families with several adults and children: school, activities, trips, groceries. There are roles: organizer, second parent, helper (nanny, grandparent) and child.

Plans

For the first 30 days everything is available without limits. After that you can continue for free in the limited «Lite» mode, or subscribe to «Family+» for the whole family: one person pays, everybody uses it. The subscription renews automatically and can be cancelled at any time in your Apple ID settings.

Languages

Русский, English, Español, Polski, Deutsch, Italiano.
