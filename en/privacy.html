1. Who we are

The app is published by [name of the person or company that publishes the app] ([country]), «we». Contact for data questions: [e-mail поддержки].

2. What data we process and why

  • Account. The sign-in method (Apple or Google), a user identifier and, if you allowed it, your email address and name. Needed so you can sign in and open your family.
  • Family data. Names and roles of family members, events, tasks, shopping lists, menus and recipes, notes about food restrictions, children's dates of birth, addresses of event places. Needed for the app to work; visible to the members of your family according to their roles.
  • Children's profiles. Created by a parent. We collect no data about children beyond what is listed above. A child on their own phone sees only their own events and tasks.
  • Avatar photos. If you add a photo from the camera or gallery, it is kept in private storage and available only to your family.
  • Location. Only when you press the SOS button yourself: one point is taken and sent to your family with the signal. There is no continuous tracking. For event addresses, coordinates are found from the address you type through Apple's map services.
  • Voice input. When you dictate, the sound is recognized by Apple's speech recognition (on the device where supported, otherwise at Apple). We do not receive or keep the recording.
  • Push notifications. Your device token, interface language and platform, to send reminders and the SOS signal.
  • Subscription. The App Store purchase identifier and the subscription's end date. We do not receive payment card data: Apple processes the payment.
  • AI menu planning. When you ask for a menu, the following is sent to the AI service: names and role of family members (adult or child), age, the food restrictions recorded, your wishes and the language, dates. The result (dish names, ingredients, steps) is saved in your family. You can leave names and restrictions out, or not use this feature.

  • Firebase (Google) — [if analytics or crash reports are enabled, describe them; otherwise delete this line].

Data may be processed outside your country; in such cases the safeguards required by applicable law are used.

4. How long data is kept

As long as your family and account exist. After an account is deleted we delete its related data within a reasonable time, except what we must keep by law.

5. Your rights and deletion

You can request access, correction, export and deletion of your data, and withdraw permissions (camera, microphone, location, notifications) in iOS settings. You can delete your account and data in the app: Settings → Account → “Delete account” (or write to [e-mail поддержки]). If you are the last member of a family, the whole family is deleted with the account; otherwise the family stays for the other members. A subscription is cancelled separately in the App Store settings. If you are in the EU, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

6. Children

The app is meant for use within a family under a parent's control. A parent creates and edits a child's profile. We show no ads to children and do not use their data for marketing. If you think we received a child's data without a parent's consent, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is sent over a secure connection; access to a family's data is limited to its members by database rules. No protection is absolute, but we use reasonable measures to protect data.

8. Changes

We may change this policy. A new version is published on this page with a date; we will tell you about material changes in the app.
