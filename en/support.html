Support

Need help, found a bug or want to suggest a feature? Write to us.

[e-mail поддержки]

Before you write

  • Read the guide and its «Frequently asked questions».
  • If notifications or the SOS signal do not arrive: check that notifications are on (iOS Settings → Notifications), there is no «Do Not Disturb» or Focus without an exception, and the app is up to date.

What to include

  • The phone model and iOS version.
  • The app version (Settings → «Version» at the bottom).
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened; a screenshot if possible.

Deleting your account and data

Write to us stating the sign-in method (Apple or Google) and we will delete the account and related data. More in the privacy policy.
